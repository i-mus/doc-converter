## Fonts embedded in PDFs made from Markdown

- **Roboto** — Apache License 2.0. Shipped inside pdfmake; used for normal text.
- **Symbols fallback** — a subset of **DejaVu Sans** (arrows, maths, box
  drawing, check marks and similar), renamed "Doc Converter Symbols" as the
  DejaVu licence requires for modified fonts. DejaVu fonts are derived from
  Bitstream Vera (© Bitstream, Inc.) with additions in the public domain; the
  licence permits free use, embedding and redistribution. See
  <https://dejavu-fonts.github.io/License.html>.
- **Emoji fallback** — a subset of **Noto Emoji** (monochrome), © Google LLC,
  SIL Open Font License 1.1. See <https://openfontlicense.org>.
- **Courier** metrics (`Courier.afm`) — © Adobe Systems Incorporated, from the
  standard PDF base-14 font metrics distributed with PDFKit.

The fallback fonts are generated by `scripts/make-pdf-fonts.py`.
